# Travel discovery domain

Use for discovery/search logic, candidate scoring, dates, airports, itinerary variants
or transport decisions. Query Graphify before inspecting the domain modules.

Keep calendar days, nights and usable travel time distinct. Compare city, nature,
roadtrip, no-car and open-jaw variants by total trip cost and real logistics, not by
flight price alone. Do not invent availability, schedules, routes or provider
capabilities; model uncertainty through source provenance.
